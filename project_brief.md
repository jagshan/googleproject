# My agent: Smart Pantry & Weather Assistant
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps users manage their pantry inventory and retrieve weather information.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User dietary preferences and location history
- Tools: Firestore inventory lookup & update (`get_pantry_items`, `add_pantry_item`), weather lookup
- Catalog/UI: Pantry items collection (name, category, quantity, expiration_date)
- Image gen: Recipe and dish visual generation
- Sandbox: n/a
